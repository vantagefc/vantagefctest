LB / RB switches tab
Admin mode adds an Admin button and a P key during matches, so you can add goals, earn coins, edit your player's stats, and more. (Mainly used for freeplay or testing)
How strongly the opposing team plays against you.
How long each half lasts in real time. The match clock still reads football minutes (45'/90').
Crowd & Commentary
Crowd roars, groans, boos and the referee's whistle are synthesized on the fly - no audio files. Captions put a live text commentary along the bottom of the pitch. Press M to mute the crowd mid-match.
Background gradient
Performance
Turns off the heavy background animations, blurs and shadows across the whole game to help it run smoothly on older or lower-powered devices. It switches on by itself when a slow device or software renderer is detected.
Ball & Jersey
Ball color and panels repaint the match ball. Jersey color repaints your team's kit in every mode - leave it at the default and your club/team colors are used instead.
My Club
Kits & Crest
Home, Away and Secondary kits repaint your side in every My Club match. The crest is drawn from your club name and active kit - set your own letters if you like.
